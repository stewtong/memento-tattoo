The handoff between sessions
A real save and load

Watch the memory survive the session.

Wednesday · 17:42> save work

No evidence in Sent Items that the briefing was delivered.

Chat was not checked. The review meeting is tomorrow at 2:00 PM.

Confirm delivery before the meeting.

Thursday · 08:05> load work

Confirm whether the briefing was delivered.

Email showed no evidence; chat remained unchecked. The review meeting is today at 2:00 PM.

The uncertainty survived with the deadline.

Today, continuity depends on what you remember. Tomorrow, it lives with the work.
